Day car rental at Shin-Fuji for the Mt Fuji area

The plan now hires a car for the day at Shin-Fuji instead of a bus or taxi (the bus runs only 3 or 4 times a day and takes about an hour). Offices near the station (Google Maps, 08:00 to 20:00 daily): Toyota Rent a Car Shinfuji Ekimae, Nippon Rent-a-Car, Nissan Rent-a-car and an ORIX Rent a Car branch. Bring a passport, home licence and International Driving Permit, and ask for snow tires.
