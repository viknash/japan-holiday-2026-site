Day 25, Sat 26 Dec: Sapporo to New Chitose Airport

JR Rapid Airport, no reservation needed, 1,230 yen. Sample departures (13:00 to 13:30 window): 13:00 Rapid Airport 88 arrives 13:46 (37 min ride plus walk to the terminal), 13:04 Section Rapid 90 arrives 13:56, 13:19 Rapid Airport 92 arrives 14:06. Trains run about every 15 minutes.

The itinerary leaves Sapporo at about 14:30 and reaches the airport at about 15:16, giving a long buffer before the 60-minute check-in deadline (17:40 for the 18:40 flight). Add margin for snow delays on the line.
