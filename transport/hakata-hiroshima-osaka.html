Connections and local legs

  • Hiroshima Station to the sights: by streetcar to Genbaku Dome-mae, about 25 minutes (my estimate, not from the planner). The Dome, Peace Memorial Museum and Orizuru Tower are within a few hundred metres of each other.
  • Plan in the itinerary: arrive Hiroshima 08:21, back at the station by about 17:15 for the 17:33 train, Shin-Osaka 18:59, hotel about 19:30 (Shin-Osaka to the Minn Umeda-North hotel is my estimate of 25 to 30 minutes).
  • Luggage: coin lockers at Hiroshima Station for the day, or forward bags to the Osaka hotel by courier. Large suitcases on the Shinkansen may need an oversized-baggage reservation (general rule; verify with JR West/JR Central).
