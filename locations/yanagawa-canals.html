Times and fares (from the company's page)

  • Hours: 09:30 to 15:00 daily. Shared boats depart every 30 minutes, 09:40 to 15:10. The ride takes about 60 minutes.
  • Shared boat: adults 2,200 yen; children aged 6 to 11 1,100 yen; 5 and under free. A reservation is not required but is recommended, and same-day tickets can sell out.
  • Private boat (reservation required): standard boat up to 16 people from 20,000 yen per boat; cushion-seat boat up to 8 people 26,000 yen. Groups of 17 or more: mail@yanagawakk.co.jp.
  • The Night Canal Cruise has ended for 2026 (back in summer 2027). The page did not give a December timetable.

Where it ends

The cruise finishes at Ohana (Okinohata area), not at the boarding point. The page extract I read did not say how to get back to the car, so ask at the ticket office.

Not checked

December timetable, how to return from Ohana, parking fees and space (the company says the lots are free), and whether Option 2 equals the company's Takahatake Park lot.
