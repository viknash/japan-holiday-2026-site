Mikaerino Falls (見帰りの滝)

Part of Day 2, Thu 3 Dec (first stop, 08:24 at the car park, 08:29 at the falls, 55 minutes).

One of Japan's 100 Best Waterfalls, in Karatsu, Saga. Flagged check conditions (mountain road).

Getting there and parking

  • Parking lot (your pin): municipal Hotaru-bashi (Firefly Bridge) parking lot, 38 spaces, toilets (WC).
  • Location 33.3664999, 130.0323035. Google Maps link for the lot and its signboard photo: <https://maps.app.goo.gl/N5VbqADyqFrNK3Wy9>
  • Fee: 500 yen per car (motorcycle 200 yen; buses 2,000 to 5,000 yen).
  • Other lots: 20 spaces near the trail entrance (municipal); 9 spaces by the dining hall Seiryu-so near the falls (no large vehicles, toilets).
  • Weekends: traffic restrictions, temporary lots, and a 100-yen shuttle bus from the Hotaru-bashi lot. Thu 3 Dec is a weekday. The sign does not say whether the weekend rules apply all year.

Walking

  • About 350 m from the lot to the trail entrance, about 800 m to the falls (each way, roughly 15 to 20 minutes).
  • Trail order from the entrance: stepping stones, rest area, Hakusen Bridge and Kagero Bridge, Ayugaeri-no-se rapids, Mikaeri-dai viewpoint, Takimi-dai platform and Momiji Bridge, Shibuki Bridge, Suzumi-dai platform, the falls.

Season

Hydrangeas peak in June and Kawazu cherry blossoms from mid-February to early March, so neither is in bloom in early December.
