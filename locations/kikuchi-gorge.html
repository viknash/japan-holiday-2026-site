Kikuchi Gorge cluster (菊池渓谷)

Part of Day 2, Thu 3 Dec, replacing Koi Fish Street. Haru, Kikuchi, Kumamoto.

On the main route you park at 15:16 and walk until about 17:11 (sunset is about 17:15); on the lower-risk alternative (skipping the Yame tea plantation) you park at 13:54 and finish about 15:49.
