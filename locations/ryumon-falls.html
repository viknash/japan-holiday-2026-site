Ryumon Falls cluster (龍門滝), Aira, Kagoshima

Part of Day 3, Fri 4 Dec, after Sogi Falls and before the drive on to Kagoshima (overnight). It is on the way from Sogi Falls (Isa) to Kagoshima.

On the main route you park at 15:17 and are back on the road at about 16:00 (35 minutes allowed for the falls, flagged check conditions), reaching Kagoshima at 16:33.
