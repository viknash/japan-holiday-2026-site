1. Do now (this week)

  • ☐ Follow up on your International Driving Permit (IDP) application. You need it, with your passport and home driving licence, for the Kyushu rental car (Wed 2 to Wed 9 Dec) and the Shin-Fuji day car (Fri 18 Dec); the rental offices will refuse the car without it. Check that it will be issued well before 2 Dec (and is valid on 18 Dec), and that it is the type Japan accepts (permits under the 1949 Geneva Convention; check with the issuing office or the Japan Automobile Federation). Put it in the same bag as your passport.
  • ☐ Nintendo Museum, Thu 17 Dec (Day 16): reservation only, no tickets at the door. Entry is by lottery, applications open about 3 months ahead, and you pay by a 3-D Secure credit card. Open the official Nintendo Museum ticket site, make a Nintendo Account, and check whether December dates are still open or whether unclaimed tickets are on sale. About 3,300 yen adult, 1,100 yen age 6-11, under 6 free. If you cannot get tickets, drop Uji and make Day 16 Nara only.
  • ☐ Universal Studios Japan, Mon 14 Dec (Day 13): buy a dated Studio Pass (the date must be chosen at purchase for visits from 1 Sept). Super Nintendo World needs a timed-entry ticket, which comes with certain Express Passes or can be taken in the app after entering the park. Decide whether to buy an Express Pass with timed entry now.
  • ☐ Ski lessons at Sapporo Teine (Sapporo option D): contact the Olympia ski school now (school@sapporo-teine.com, +81-11-685-7000, 9:00 to 16:00). Ask about English lessons for 22 to 25 Dec (English group lessons for first-timers were listed at about 21,000 yen per person for a full day, minimum 2 people, age 7 and over; private lessons from age 4; full payment in advance), and whether the family can share a lesson. Japanese-language group lessons were 7,000 or 9,000 yen. Winter-holiday junior classes opened for registration on 9 Oct (some by lottery). These are from a search result and a guide, so confirm everything; the school's English page returned an error.
  • ☐ Rental car, Wed 2 Dec to Wed 9 Dec: pick up at Fukuoka Airport (you land 07:35; plan about 09:45 collection), return at Hakata (Fukuoka city) on Wed 9 Dec. Ask for: child seat if the child is under 6 *(general guidance, verify)*, an ETC card or expressway pass for foreign visitors, winter tyres or chains for the Aso, Kuju and Takachiho days (6 to 8 Dec), and a one-way or different-location return if needed.
  • ☐ International Driving Permit or the licence-translation your country requires for Japan *(general guidance, verify for your licence)*. Bring your home licence too.
  • ☐ Accommodation for all 24 nights (table below). Check free-cancellation dates.
  • ☐ Passports valid, entry paperwork done (Visit Japan Web registration is recommended before arrival *(general guidance, verify)*).
  • ☐ Travel insurance, eSIM or pocket Wi-Fi, some yen cash, a card that works in Japan, an IC card (Suica or ICOCA) or phone IC card.

4. Closer to the date (2 to 3 weeks before)

  • ☐ Check live road status and weather for the mountain days: Takachiho (Routes 218/325), Aso caldera rim, Kuju plateau and Kokonoe, Bungo-ono and Usa inland falls, Yabakei. Decide whether to use the lower-risk alternatives written into the itinerary.
  • ☐ Opening hours and winter operation: Sengan-en, Sakurajima ferry timetable (a car ferry, no reservation), Kirishima Factory Garden, Beppu Hells (usually close about 17:00), Kumamoto Castle, Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum, Mt Rokko cable car last ride, Shirogane Blue Pond access.
  • ☐ Download offline Google Maps for each region and save the route-map links from the itinerary.
  • ☐ Online check-in for the Scoot flight TR 893 on 26 Dec and the Vietnam Airlines flights VN 656 / VN 350 on 1-2 Dec; baggage: outbound 23 kg first bag, return 25 kg each.
  • ☐ Oversized luggage on the Shinkansen: large suitcases may need an oversized-baggage seat reservation *(general guidance, verify)*. Consider courier forwarding (takkyubin) between Hakata and the Osaka hotel, and Osaka to Tokyo.

5. Packing

  • ☐ Layers for 0 to 15 degrees on Kyushu mountain days; proper winter clothing and boots for Hokkaido (22 to 26 Dec).
  • ☐ Power adapter (Japan is Type A), medicines, a child's comfort items, spare phone battery, a small day bag for the Rokko and Hakodate night views.
  • ☐ Printouts or screenshots of tickets and QR codes; offline copies of the timetable files.

6. Day before and day of

  • ☐ 1 Dec: check-in, hand luggage, flight 20:45 from Changi.
  • ☐ 2 Dec: after landing 07:35, immigration, baggage, car pick-up (allow about 2 hours).
  • ☐ Each morning: re-check the day's route map and any "check conditions" stops.
