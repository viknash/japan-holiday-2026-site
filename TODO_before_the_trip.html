1. Do now (this week)

  • ☐ Follow up on your International Driving Permit (IDP) application. You need it, with your passport and home driving licence, for the Kyushu rental car (Wed 2 to Wed 9 Dec) and the Shin-Fuji day car (Fri 18 Dec); the rental offices will refuse the car without it. Check that it will be issued well before 2 Dec and is valid on 18 Dec.
  • ☐ Check your IDP is the type Japan accepts. Permits issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention are accepted; check with the issuing office or the Japan Automobile Federation *(general guidance, verify)*.
  • ☐ Pack the IDP with your passport and home driving licence.
  • ☐ Collect the Nintendo Museum QR tickets (booked: Wed 16 Dec, entry 15:00-15:30, Uji): they can be collected from 14:00 JST on Tue 15 Dec on the Nintendo Museum ticket page. Credit-card tickets cannot be cancelled once the QR codes are issued. Paid: 2 adults and 1 child, 7,700 yen.
  • ☐ Save the Nintendo Museum QR tickets to your phone (screenshot as a backup) before leaving the hotel on Wed 16 Dec (Day 15). Venue: 56 Ogura-cho, Uji City, Kyoto (Ogura Station, Kintetsu Kyoto Line); be there before 15:00.
  • ☐ Universal Studios Japan, Mon 14 Dec (Day 13): buy a dated Studio Pass (the date must be chosen at purchase for visits from 1 Sept).
  • ☐ Super Nintendo World timed-entry ticket. It comes with certain Express Passes, or can be taken in the app after entering the park.
  • ☐ Decide whether to buy an Express Pass with timed entry now.
  • ☐ Ski lessons at Sapporo Teine (Sapporo option D): contact the Olympia ski school now (school@sapporo-teine.com, +81-11-685-7000, 9:00 to 16:00). These details are from a search result and a guide, so confirm everything; the school's English page returned an error.
  • ☐ Ask the ski school about English lessons for 22 to 25 Dec. English group lessons for first-timers were listed at about 21,000 yen per person for a full day, minimum 2 people, age 7 and over; private lessons from age 4; full payment in advance. Japanese-language group lessons were 7,000 or 9,000 yen.
  • ☐ Ask whether the family can share a lesson.
  • ☐ Check winter-holiday junior class registration. It opened on 9 Oct (some classes by lottery).
  • ☐ Rental car, Wed 2 Dec to Wed 9 Dec: pick up at Fukuoka Airport (you land 07:35; plan about 09:45 collection), return at Hakata (Fukuoka city) on Wed 9 Dec.
  • ☐ Rental car: ask for winter tyres or chains for the Aso, Kuju and Takachiho days (6 to 8 Dec).
  • ☐ Rental car: ask for an ETC card or expressway pass for foreign visitors.
  • ☐ Rental car: confirm the different-location return (airport pick-up, Hakata drop-off).
  • ☐ Rental car: child seat only if the child is under 6 *(general guidance, verify)*.
  • ☐ Book accommodation for all 24 nights (table in section 3).
  • ☐ Check the free-cancellation dates on each hotel booking.
  • ☐ Passports valid for all three travellers.
  • ☐ Entry paperwork: register on Visit Japan Web (Japan's official immigration and customs pre-registration site) before arrival; recommended, not checked as required *(general guidance, verify)*.
  • ☐ Travel insurance for all three travellers.
  • ☐ eSIM or pocket Wi-Fi.
  • ☐ Some yen cash.
  • ☐ A card that works in Japan.
  • ☐ An IC card (Suica or ICOCA) or a phone IC card.
