Title: Japan's 100 Best Waterfalls: Mikaeri Falls (Mikaeri-no-taki), Guide Map

Distances (from the municipal Hotaru-bashi parking lot)

  • To the promenade (walking trail) entrance: about 350 m
  • To Mikaeri Falls: about 800 m

Parking fees (駐車料金):

  • Standard car: 500 yen; motorcycle: 200 yen
  • Large bus: 5,000 yen; medium bus: 3,500 yen
  • Small bus / micro-bus: 2,000 yen

Weekends (土日):

  • "On weekends, traffic restrictions apply from here. Go to the temporary parking lots." (arrow at the west end of the map)
  • "On weekends, a shuttle bus runs from here (100 yen one way)." (arrow at the Hotaru-bashi parking lot)
  • The sign does not say whether the weekend arrangements apply all year or only in the hydrangea season. Thu 3 Dec 2026 is a weekday.

Seasons

  • Hydrangeas (あじさい): best viewing early June to late June. Note: traffic restrictions apply during the Hydrangea Festival.
  • Kawazu cherry blossoms (河津桜): best viewing mid-February to early March.
  • Neither is in bloom in early December.
