Goryokaku Tower and Park (suggested)

43-9 Goryōkakuchō, Hakodate, Hokkaido 040-0001, Japan

Goryokaku Tower is a 107-metre tower with an observatory giving views of the mountains and of the star-shaped fort below. The fort, Goryokaku, was built in the 1860s, and the park around it is a nice place to walk.

We visited early in the morning to beat the crowds. Even then, there was still a queue for the handful of prime spots that offer the best view of Goryokaku’s iconic star shape, along with fantastic views of Hakodate. If you have time, take a walk through Goryokaku Park afterward. The aerial view from the tower is impressive, but seeing the fort from ground level gives you a completely different perspective and makes the visit feel more complete.
