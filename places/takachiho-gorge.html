Takachiho Gorge

Mukoyama, Takachiho, Nishiusuki District, Miyazaki 882-1103, Japan

River canyon with waterfalls over sheer basalt cliffs, viewable from rented boats & a paved path.

Open in Google Maps (street view, more photos and reviews there)

The most scenic gorge anywhere , especially if you can reserve one of the rowing boats and see it from below. The reservation opens two weeks ahead and usually filled up immediately. I tried several times and was unsuccessful, but luckily I got a last minute cancellation the day before. Everybody wanted to park at lot 1 , but actually it was nicer to park at lot 2 and enjoy the very scenic walk to the fall. Just be aware that if they had heavy rain a day or two before, the water will look muddy and not pretty, plus the boat ride will close as well .
